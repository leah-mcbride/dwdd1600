Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include:

John Hancock
As President of the Continental Congress, Hancock is widely recognized for his large and prominent signature on the Declaration. His bold signing became a symbol of courage and commitment to American independence.
Thomas Jefferson
The principal author of the Declaration of Independence, Jefferson's eloquent writing provided a philosophical foundation emphasizing liberty, natural rights, and the pursuit of happiness.
Benjamin Franklin
A highly respected statesman, inventor, and diplomat, Franklin helped negotiate crucial alliances for the fledgling nation and lent his wisdom and prestige to the Continental Congress.
John Adams
A strong advocate for independence from Britain, Adams played a key role in persuading Congress to adopt the Declaration and later became the second President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
Known as a leader of the Patriot movement in Massachusetts, he was instrumental in organizing resistance to British policies and inspiring colonial unity in pursuit of independence.
Roger Sherman
Contributed politically to the Revolutionary cause and continued to serve in the new United States government.
Robert Morris
Played a key financial role in supporting the Revolutionary effort and helped establish the financial foundations of the new nation.
Charles Carroll
Actively supported independence from Maryland and provided political leadership in the formation of the United States.
George Wythe
A skilled legal scholar and mentor, Wythe contributed to the political and legal framework of the emerging nation.

These key figures played pivotal roles in American independence and early governance.
