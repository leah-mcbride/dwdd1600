Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, ethical, and safe use of technology to engage, communicate, and participate in digital spaces.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law—also called cyberlaw or IT law—applies traditional legal concepts to the digital environment. It governs how digital information is created, stored, transmitted, and used.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy, a formal document that defines the acceptable and unacceptable use of an organization's technology resources such as computers, networks, and internet services.

What is plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's work, ideas, or words without proper acknowledgment, presenting them as your own.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty refers to any form of cheating or unethical behavior in academic work that violates principles of integrity and honesty.

What are the rules of AI Generated Content?

Acceptable: Using AI as a research aid, brainstorming tool, or drafting assistant, provided the student's own work is the final product and properly credited

Unacceptable: Submitting AI-generated work as if it were entirely the student's own, without disclosure or citation, especially for assignments that require original thought or personal insight
