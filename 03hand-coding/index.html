Synopsis

Plot

When Sawyer- an unemployed, out-of-school 19 year old with none of her future planned out- decides to accept a crew member position at a mysterious ice cream parlor called Cupid's Bowl, she realizes things like monsters and magic might not be as impossible as she once thought.

While defeating monsters alongside her coworkers Charlotte and Valerie, Sawyer discovers more secrets about the world around her, and why she was called upon as a magical girl. She also learns the truth about who she really is, and the kind of person she wants to become.

Genre

  1. First and foremost, Cupid's Bowl is a magical girl story, a genre that originates from anime and manga.
  2. Cupid's Bowl will also share characteristics of other genres, such as slice-of-life, comedy, and maybe even some romance as well.
