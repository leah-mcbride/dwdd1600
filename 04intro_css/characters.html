Characters

Main "Neapolitan" Trio

Sawyer Bell
Our main-est main character and the last to join the trio. Awkward when you first get to know her but well-meaning. Her magical girl form is inspired by strawberry ice cream.
Charlotte Carnella
Our first trio member to join Cupid's Bowl. Simultaneously the coolest and nerdiest person you know. Her magical girl form is inspired by chocolate ice cream.
Valerie Escaran
Started working at Cupid's Bowl soon after Charlotte. You can't really tell if she actually hates you or if she's just really committed to the bit. Her magical girl form is inspired by vanilla ice cream.
